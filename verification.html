Verification & Trust

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Profiles are not automatically verified

A profile appearing on DEWIFY does not by itself prove that the person's identity, qualifications, employer, company affiliation, work history or claims are genuine.

Future verification

Production DEWIFY may introduce optional verification for identity, email, phone, company ownership, professional credentials or other signals. A verification badge should describe exactly what was checked and when.

Do not fake verification

Users must not claim that DEWIFY has verified something when it has not. Do not upload forged certificates, identity documents, badges or screenshots.

Employers and workers

Employers should verify candidates before making decisions, and candidates should verify employers and job offers before accepting them. DEWIFY is a discovery platform, not a substitute for normal due diligence.
