DEWIFY

Safety Rules

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Be honest

Do not fake your identity, age, skills, education, work history, company affiliation, job availability, compensation or other material information.

Verify before you trust

Before accepting work, sending money, sharing sensitive information, meeting someone, or relying on a job listing, independently verify the person or company and the terms. DEWIFY does not guarantee the identity or claims of users.

Keep sensitive information private

Never publish passwords, one-time codes, banking credentials, government identification numbers or other sensitive credentials in your profile, job listing, comments or messages.

Report abuse

Report scams, impersonation, fake jobs, harassment, threats, suspicious requests, stolen content and other violations through the production reporting channel. Preserve relevant evidence and avoid escalating a dangerous interaction.

Account enforcement

DEWIFY may restrict, suspend or remove accounts and listings that appear to violate these safety rules or applicable law.

Marketplace disclaimer

DEWIFY is a platform for discovery and communication. Users are responsible for their own decisions and due diligence. If someone lies about material information and another user relies on that lie, DEWIFY does not accept responsibility for the consequences to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law.

These rules are a product draft and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before launch.
