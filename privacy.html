DEWIFY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, DEWIFY may collect account information such as name, email address, date of birth or age, profile picture, location, skills, biography, experience, company details, job listings, applications, messages and technical information needed to operate the service.

2. Why we use information

Information may be used to create and display profiles, provide search and matching features, operate accounts, communicate with users, prevent abuse, secure the service, improve the product and meet legal obligations.

3. Profile visibility

Information you choose to place on a public or searchable profile may be visible to other users. Do not publish passwords, financial credentials, government identification numbers or other sensitive information in public profile fields.

4. Profile pictures

Only upload images you have permission to use. Production storage and processing of profile pictures should use secure, access-controlled infrastructure.

5. Retention and deletion

Production DEWIFY should provide account deletion and appropriate data deletion or retention controls. Data may need to be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, disputes, or legal obligations.

6. Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should protect their credentials and avoid publishing sensitive information.

7. Third parties

The production service may use infrastructure providers and other processors to host data, deliver authentication, store files, provide analytics, or operate communications. Applicable disclosures should identify relevant providers when the service launches.

8. Your choices

Production users should be given appropriate ways to access, correct, update, or request deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

9. India-first launch

DEWIFY is designed initially around India. The final production privacy notice should be reviewed against applicable Indian data-protection requirements before collecting real user data.

This document is a product draft and is not legal advice. Have qualified counsel review it before launch.
